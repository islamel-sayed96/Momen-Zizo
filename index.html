    About

    Behind the lens

    I'm Momen Abd Elhady, a videographer, photographer and editor. I've been capturing weddings, events and brands since 2010, with a focus on lighting, composition and telling the story of the moment.

    I started in Alexandria and now work from Dubai, shooting for agencies, brands and private clients. Along the way I've photographed concerts for artists including Tamer Hosny, Tamer Ashour, Hamaki and Bahaa Sultan, and covered conferences and exhibitions such as BIG 5 and Dubai Derma.
